Linear Algebra Quick Formulas
  • RREF: $[A | \mathbf{b}] \rightarrow [I | \mathbf{x}]$
  • Dot Product: $\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v} = \|\mathbf{u}\|\|\mathbf{v}\|\cos\theta$
  • Gram-Schmidt: $\mathbf{u}_k = \mathbf{v}_k - \sum \text{proj}$
  • Diagonalization: $A = P D P^{-1}$
